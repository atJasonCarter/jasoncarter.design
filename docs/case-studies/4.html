Making our most useful data actionable

Audience targeting analytics for more than glancing

Role Head of Product Design
Timeline 5 months · 2019
Platform Web application
Team 1 designer, 1 engineer, 1 PM

Final product: comparing two audience segments

01

The problem

We were a small shop. but we were hitting pain points as we grew. all our manual processes needed to scale. Our main product was helping customers choose the right audience for their ads. We had a lot of data, but interpretting it felt like reading tea leaves, and then our back office (me) had to go manually put the audience together for the customer.

We needed to make our most useful data actionable.

  • Kill the manual processes
  • Current data only compared audience vs the US census
  • Users should be able to form a hypothesis and test if that audience is worthwhile
Opportunity: increase revenue for us and our customers by making their audiences more accurate

Existing state: One long scroll of data, comparing data to find outliers is tedious.

02

Research & discovery

As a startup we were all doing sales, and by the point my PM and I prioritized this product, we had talked to over 200 customers and had a ton of qualitative data about how we could improve our current offering.

Marketing Managers
Need to quickly identify and target specific audience segments, build audiences, and test them quickly to reapidly itterate on their marketing strategy.
Executives
Understand where their products' audience excells or lags behind compared to their competitors.
Key insight: Make comparison a key differentiator, make it easy.
03

Design principles

Actionable
I've said `actionable` 100 times in this case study, but this was a formative moment for my design career of learning how to make useful products.
Sort widgets by use, but let users find the tools they need to do their job.
Instructional
Customization must be obvious and immediate, users should learn they have access to more than they knew.

Design decisions

Outcomes

06

Reflection

Biggest surprise
Geniunely how useful it would be, our adopters made over 20x the ammount of audiences, and invested far more than their partners, i initially thought this would be primarily aesthetics.
Would redesign
Theres a lot. I overly focused on making everything fit onto one screen. I now believe some scrolling and making pagination views would make the product better. the filters and selectors could have been a modal or side bar as well.
What's next
More data, changing the layout to be more customizable (so audiences could compare `demographics` and `app installs` for example instead of divide tabs).
